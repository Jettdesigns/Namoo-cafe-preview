About Us

A proper barbershop, not a rushed trim

"Golden Blade GC barbershop offers clean fades, sharp haircuts, beard grooming, and modern men’s styling in a professional and welcoming atmosphere. Our skilled barbers focus on precision, quality, and attention to detail to keep every client looking fresh, confident, and sharp."

01
Inside Paradise Centre on Cavill Ave — two minutes from the beach, easy to find, easy to park near.
02
Open seven days a week, with walk-ins welcome alongside bookings.
03
Led by owner Ali, with a team that includes Kash, Xavier, Matin, Paul and Evan behind the chair.
