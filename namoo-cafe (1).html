The story

Grown from one small café.

NaMoo Cafe is owned by Young Nam, who grew up in Korea, and his wife. Before Gallery Walk, they ran a café in a touristy corner of South Australia — years of experience they eventually brought up to Tamborine Mountain, where NaMoo has now been part of the Gallery Walk strip for several years.

Alongside their team, they built a menu around folding Korean flavours into familiar café classics: a beef burger with a Korean twist, spicy fried chicken and kimchi fried rice sitting comfortably next to homemade scones and Devonshire tea.

Namoo (나무) means tree in Korean — a fitting name for a café sitting in the rainforest air of Tamborine Mountain.
